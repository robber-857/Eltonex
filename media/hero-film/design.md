# ELTONEX — Gold Horizon

A monumental ELTONEX rocket hovers above a dark basalt landscape at a warm horizon. The exact brand silhouette is the centrepiece, with a deep graphite body, substantial milled edges, champagne reflections and yellow-gold diagonal/flame details.

## Composition

- Original scene inspired by the **staging** of the supplied [Opo Finance reference](https://dribbble.com/shots/27285977-Opo-Finance-landing-page-web-design-hero-section-3D-Animation): monumental brand object to the right, low landscape, luminous horizon, grounded shadow and quiet copy area to the left.
- This is a new ELTONEX model and scene. No reference artwork, video frames, proprietary model or texture is embedded in the deliverable.
- The sculpture occupies about 54% of the frame height, centred near 73% horizontal / 47% vertical. The left 45% remains quiet for live website text.
- The original SVG paths define the XY outline. Extrusion, chamfers and a shallow Z-axis crown supply mass and soft face reflections. The gold inlay sits clearly in front of the crowned graphite surface.
- Ground is a procedural, finely textured basalt/sand surface with low distant ridges. No freestanding low-poly pebbles, particles, grids, HUD, stars or decorative ribbon forms.
- A continuous charcoal-to-warm horizon, physical studio reflection panels and restrained ground light keep the scene luminous without a large white pool.
- No typography is burned into the movie. “Build what's next.” and the original ELTONEX lettering remain the website's live content.

## Motion and delivery

- One uninterrupted 12-second shot: subtle periodic pitch/yaw, ten-centimetre levitation, travelling studio highlights and ground illumination. Camera drift is barely perceptible.
- All state comes from a modulo-12-second phase; source frames at 0 and 12 seconds are identical.
- Silent 1600×900 H.264/yuv420p at 24fps, 288 encoded frames, with MP4 fast start.
- The website plays the finished movie and does not run this authoring scene or load its Three.js dependencies.
